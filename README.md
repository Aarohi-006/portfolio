# Aarohi Lal — Portfolio

Portfolio website for **Aarohi Lal**, a Communication Design student (BFA, 2028) at Parsons School of Design, The New School, in New York. I design UX/UI, branding, editorial work and fine art.

**Live site:** https://aarohi-006.github.io/portfolio/

## What's inside

- **UX/UI Design:** The New School Campus App, The City That Speaks in Layers website, and UI Worlds, each linking to a live prototype
- **Co-design at Instagram:** a two-day, 12-hour co-design session at Instagram's New York office (Oct 2026)
- **Brand & Pitch:** Lost in the Sauce (Chipotle × YouTube) and the Summer Fridays Hydration Bar
- **Editorial Design:** The City That Speaks in Layers book and the Feminism Wikibook
- **Posters & Layouts:** posters and Illustrator studies
- **Fine Art:** a colored pencil self-portrait, paintings and sculpture
- **About & Contact**

## Interactions

- A kolam you can draw by moving the cursor (or dragging a finger) across the dots in the hero
- Rangoli-inspired dividers, a block-print pattern and a marigold toran above the contact section
- Neon swirls that drift in layers as you scroll
- Images that lift with layered pink and marigold offsets when you hover over them
- A gallery wall of framed art that swings when you hover
- A photo collage that spreads apart when you hover
- A lightbox with Prev/Next and arrow-key navigation
- A cursor glow (desktop only)

The site respects the "reduced motion" setting and works on phones.

## Built with

- HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single `index.html` file, with no frameworks or build step
- Google Fonts: Schibsted Grotesk, Hanken Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono, Tiro Devanagari Hindi and Yellowtail
- Designed by Aarohi Lal and coded with the help of an AI coding assistant

## Run it locally

Download or clone the repository and open `index.html` in a browser. Keep the `img` folder next to `index.html` so the images load.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Upload `index.html`, `README.md` and the `img` folder to the repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set the source to **Deploy from a branch**, then choose **main** and **/ (root)**.
4. Click **Save**. After a minute or two, the site is live at `https://aarohi-006.github.io/portfolio/`.
